Intervention leverage decision-support tool

Supplementary file S3 to “From mapping to mitigation: A closed-form intervention leverage measure for healthcare supply chain risk networks”. The tool uses the aggregated direct-relation matrix of the study (Table 5) and reproduces the leverage (Eq. 14), target reduction (Eq. 15), break-even ratios and exact bundle enumeration of the paper. Enter relative intervention costs and a budget to obtain the budget-optimal bundle and the cost–reduction frontier. Everything runs locally in the browser.
